WHO WE ARE

ABOUT GENERAL TUNA

GENERAL TUNA is a student-led food business offering a creative take on a familiar and versatile ingredient: tuna.

Our featured product, GENERAL TUNA's Tunaballs, combines tuna with potatoes, vegetables, seasonings, and cheese to create a crispy, bite-sized snack.

Customers can choose between two available flavors: Regular and Spicy, each offering a different option for their snacking preference.

Through this business venture, we aim to apply our entrepreneurial knowledge and skills in product development, marketing, customer engagement, and business management.
